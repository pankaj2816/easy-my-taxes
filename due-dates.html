Statutory Penalties for Delayed Indian Tax Filings

Missing deadlines can lead to heavy compounding interest, late filing fees, and loss of tax benefits.

⚠️

Section 234F Late Fee

Filing ITR after the 31st July statutory deadline attracts a mandatory late fee of ₹5,000 (₹1,000 for total income up to ₹5 Lakhs) under Section 234F.

● Cannot carry forward stock/F&O trading losses if filed late.
📉

Section 234A/B/C Interest

Mandatory simple interest of 1% per month on unpaid tax dues. Section 234A applies to late return filing, while 234B & 234C penalize advance tax shortfalls.

● Compounded monthly until final tax discharge.
🏢

GST Late Fees & 18% Interest

Delayed filing of GSTR-3B incurs a late fee of ₹50/day (₹20/day for Nil returns) plus 18% per annum interest on net cash tax liability.

● E-Way bill generation blocked if returns delayed for 2 consecutive periods.
⚖️

MCA DIR-3 & Annual Defaults

Delayed Director KYC (DIR-3 KYC) attracts a mandatory ₹5,000 fine per director and DIN deactivation. Late ROC filing (AOC-4/MGT-7) attracts ₹100/day.

● Risk of company strike-off and director disqualification.
📑

Section 234E TDS Late Filing

Delay in submitting quarterly TDS/TCS statements (Form 24Q/26Q) incurs a statutory late fee of ₹200 per day until the return is furnished.

● Penalty under Section 271H ranging from ₹10,000 to ₹1,00,000.
