Everything you need to know about tax regimes, deductions, deadlines, and working with CA Pradeep Agarwal.

For AY 2026-27, the New Tax Regime is the default regime with a ₹75,000 standard deduction for salaried individuals and zero tax for taxable incomes up to ₹7.75 Lakhs. However, if you have total deductions exceeding ₹3.75 Lakhs (via 80C, 80D, HRA, NPS, and Home Loan interest under Section 24b), the Old Regime may still save you more. Test both with our free AY 2026-27 Calculator above!
The statutory due date for individuals and salaried taxpayers is 31st July 2026. For taxpayers requiring a statutory tax audit under Section 44AB, the due date is 31st October 2026. Belated returns can be filed until 31st December 2026 with a late fee under Section 234F.
Under Section 44ADA, eligible professionals (gross receipts up to ₹75 Lakhs) can declare 50% of their gross revenue as profit and pay tax only on that 50%, with zero requirement to maintain detailed accounting vouchers or undergo statutory audits.
