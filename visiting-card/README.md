## 🖨️ Commercial Print Specifications

- **Finished Card Size (Trim Size):** `3.50 in × 2.00 in` (88.9 mm × 50.8 mm)
- **Bleed Size:** `3.75 in × 2.25 in` (95.25 mm × 57.15 mm) with 0.125 in (3.175 mm) margin
- **Recommended Paper Stock:** 350 GSM or 400 GSM Matte Velvet / Silk Touch Art Card
- **Recommended Finish:** Soft-Touch Velvet Matte Lamination + Metallic Gold Foil Stamping on typography &amp; monogram
- **Compliance:** 100% compliant with the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 and ICAI Code of Ethics

## 🚀 How to Print or Save as PDF

1. Double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome or Edge.
2. Select your preferred color theme (**Royal Navy &amp; Gold**, **Pearl &amp; Emerald**, or **Obsidian Platinum**).
3. Select your layout mode:
   - **Single Card:** For printing 1 card duplex (Page 1 = Front, Page 2 = Back).
   - **A4 Sheet (10-Up):** For printing 10 cards per A4 sheet with corner trim/crop marks.
4. Press `Ctrl + P` (or click "Print / Save as PDF"):
   - Destination: **Save as PDF** (or select your printer).
   - Margins: **None**.
   - Scale: **100% (Default)**.
   - Options: **Background graphics CHECKED**.
